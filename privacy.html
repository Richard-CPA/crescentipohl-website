Privacy Statement

Last updated: 2025

Data protection and our commitment

Crescenti Pohl Advisory is governed by EU data protection law. We understand the importance of your privacy rights, and we are committed to treating any personal data or information legally collected by us or provided to us with respect and care.

What data we may collect

We might collect, process, and use personal information for the purpose of providing services to our clients, or alternatively for contacting and/or site experience improvement purposes. This information might include but is not limited to:

  • Names, addresses, email addresses, and phone numbers
  • Demographic information
  • Financial information
  • Operational processes and business practices

How we share your data

We do not share any of this information with third parties, except in the following circumstances:

  • Where you have given us your written or verbal consent
  • Where it is needed by a third party to provide services on your behalf
  • Where it is required by law

Third-party links

The present site may contain external links to content provided by third parties, such as articles, multimedia, or other sources of information. Crescenti Pohl Advisory warns that these external sites might be governed by different data protection regulations, and we disclaim any responsibility concerning the data and privacy protection practices of these third parties.

Cookies policy

Cookies are small text files containing information about your web visits and activity, user preferences, and login information. Under normal circumstances, cookies cannot transfer viruses or malware to your computer.

Once you have given your consent for the use of cookies, you have the right to withdraw it (opt-out) at any time by deleting cookies through your browser settings. More information on cookies can be found at www.allaboutcookies.org.

How we use cookies

With your consent, our website may use cookies for the following purposes:

Preferences

To enhance your user experience by remembering your preferences and settings across visits.

Secure access

To provide features such as accessing secure areas for registered users.

Localisation

To identify your region and language in order to serve appropriate content.

Analytics

For marketing analytics purposes to understand how our site is used and improve it.

Questions about this policy

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this privacy statement or the way we process your personal data, please do not hesitate to contact us.
