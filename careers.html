Career progression

Where you could fit

We hire across all experience levels. Wherever you are in your career, we will find the right scope of work and responsibility to match your profile.

A
Analyst

Entry level. Strong degree, early career experience, and a demonstrated interest in energy and consulting. Works on defined project workstreams supported by senior colleagues.

C
Consultant

Typically a PhD or ~3 years of relevant experience. Leads workstreams independently and rapidly builds subject matter expertise in their domain.

M
Manager

Leads projects and small teams. Recognised by clients for deep knowledge. Experienced in managing delivery end-to-end and reporting to client leadership.

AP
Associate Partner

Acquires and delivers projects. A long-standing, trusted client partner with deep subject matter expertise and business development capability.

Growth & learning

Continuous professional development

We invest in our people across all three dimensions of professional growth. From day one, you develop through real client exposure, internal knowledge sharing, and a flexible budget for external training.

On-the-job training

  • Apply methods directly in real project environments
  • Receive structured feedback from clients and project managers
  • Build and deepen subject matter expertise in the energy sector

Internal training

  • Deep-dives, workshops and self-guided study programmes
  • Knowledge exchange from project experience and internal topic leadership
  • Methodical and subject matter best practices

External training

  • Online courses, certifications and in-person workshops
  • Access to external subject matter experts for methodical topics
  • Fully supported where relevant to industry and business consulting
