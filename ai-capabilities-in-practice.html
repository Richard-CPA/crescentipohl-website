AI capabilities in practice

We don't just advise on AI. We build with it.

We've embedded AI across how we work and what we deliver — from how our consultants operate day-to-day, to the tools we build and run for clients.

What we observe

Where clients are today

Across engagements, the same three constraints come up again and again — in people, in process, and in the underlying data.

01

People & Skills

Large variation in confidence and willingness to experiment. Some teams adopt AI overnight — others don't trust it. Clients tell us they need coaching, clearer permissions, and defined use cases before they can move.

02

Governance & Processes

IT policy frequently blocks access to the best tools. Approval cycles are too slow for fast-moving market opportunities. Unclear risk frameworks leave teams paralysed rather than empowered.

03

Infrastructure & Data

Large ETRMs dominate but good quality data extraction remains difficult. Data quality must be right before AI can be applied to risk and trading. Cloud and SaaS are now the standard — but integration remains the hard part.
