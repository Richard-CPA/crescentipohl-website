Energy Transition

The energy transition is motivated by the reduction of greenhouse gases to lessen climate change. The core remains the build-up of renewable energy generation. Challenges and emerging topics include energy storage, hydrogen and ammonium, flexibility provision, energy security, and grid stability.

Business Transformation & Digitalization

European utilities are accelerating their transformation to meet rising customer expectations, regulatory demands, and sustainability goals. Transformation is an ongoing journey demanding continuous innovation, technology adaptation, and organizational agility while keeping operational efficiency.

03

Market Insight & Strategy

In today's volatile energy market, European utilities need to stay attentive, vigilant, and agile. Successful utilities invest in data-driven insights and flexible strategies to anticipate change and respond proactively. Those who can adapt quickly with fast time-to-market are best positioned.

Data & Artificial Intelligence

The power of data and AI is reshaping the energy industry, driving efficiency and unlocking new potential. Real-time data quality requires robust governance. Introducing AI carefully and responsibly will unlock its potential while avoiding risks around privacy and proprietary strategic insight.
