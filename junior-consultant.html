Junior Management
Consultant

Entry level Permanent · Full-time Cyprus · United Kingdom · Germany

About Crescenti Pohl Advisory

We are a small but strongly connected business consultancy working with some of the largest European energy companies. We have started to grow into a wider recognised brand and currently have a physical presence in three countries: Cyprus, United Kingdom, and Germany.

We are now aiming to further expand our footprint by growing our team across all those countries. We are looking for highly motivated candidates to join us as Junior Management Consultant — a great career-starter opportunity for ambitious professionals looking to work and thrive at the forefront of the European energy sector alongside our senior business consultants and market specialists with deep industry knowledge.

What we are looking for

  • A fast learner and capable of understanding highly complex topics
  • An adept problem-solver with critical thinking and attention to detail
  • An excellent communicator with strong presentation skills
  • An expert in collecting, analysing, and interpreting information
  • Willing to travel within Europe if required

The role

You will be developing concepts for change and supporting our utility clients in large business transformations. Our projects span the full breadth of the European energy sector — from energy transition and renewables to data platforms, trading IT, and market strategy.

Project examples can be found on our website at www.crescentipohl.com.

What we offer

📈
Career prospects Permanent employment with great career prospects and success-based promotions
💰
Compensation Above average base salary and extra performance-based bonus
🏖️
Annual leave 25 days of annual leave per year
🎓
Training budget Flexible training budget to support your professional development

How to apply

Please send your application to recruitment@crescentipohl.com. In your cover letter, please address the following three points:

1
What fascinates you in business consulting and why you want to build your career in the energy sector
2
How your experience and training enable you to be a good business consultant
3
What you need to keep a good work-life balance and stay motivated in a demanding environment

We are looking forward to hearing from you.

Your future colleagues at Crescenti Pohl Advisory
