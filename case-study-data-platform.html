Strategic Cloud
Data Platform

Replacing a fragmented on-premise landscape with a scalable, cloud-based data integration and analytics platform — built iteratively and scaled through continuous delivery.

Context

Data is increasingly central to competitive advantage in European energy markets — enabling better trading decisions, more precise asset management, and faster regulatory reporting. Yet many utilities operate on data architectures built incrementally over decades, resulting in fragmented on-premise landscapes where scalability is limited, integration is fragile, and analytical work depends on manual processes.

This client had reached a tipping point: the on-premise landscape could no longer support the analytical ambitions of the business, manual data workarounds had proliferated to the point of operational risk, and a strategic decision had been made to build a modern, cloud-native data platform from the ground up.

"When manual data workarounds become part of the operational process, the data architecture has stopped serving the business and started constraining it."

Crescenti Pohl Advisory · Project observation

Client situation

The starting point
  • On-premise system landscape with applications that could not scale to meet growing analytical demand
  • Data processing reliant on manual triggers and extensive human intervention at each stage
  • Legacy processes had accumulated significant manual workarounds, creating operational risk
The strategic decision
  • Decision taken to build a strategic, cloud-based data integration and analytics platform
  • New platform required to replace manual processes with automated, governed data pipelines
  • Architecture needed to support both current operational needs and future AI/ML capability

Our approach

Solution design & architecture
Backlog definition & prioritisation
Data capabilities development (Ingest · Transform · Orchestrate)
Continuous scaling & operational improvement

Our contribution

Initial solution design and cloud architecture

Produced the end-to-end solution design and cloud architecture for the new platform — defining storage tiers, integration patterns, data flows, and the technical stack — providing the blueprint from which all subsequent delivery work was organised.

Structured backlog at epic, feature and story level

Defined the full delivery backlog from epics down to user stories, with clear prioritisation criteria and a structured process for backlog refinement — ensuring the team always worked on the highest-value, best-understood items.

Core data capabilities development

Developed the foundational data capabilities including ingestion connectors for all source systems, transformation and enrichment logic, and an orchestration layer managing pipeline scheduling, dependency resolution and monitoring.

Operational capabilities scaled through continuous delivery

Established the operational model for the platform — runbooks, incident management, data quality monitoring — and scaled capabilities continuously through iterative development cycles, progressively replacing manual processes with automated, governed pipelines.

Outcome

The client moved from a fragmented, manually-operated data landscape to a governed, automated cloud platform capable of supporting the analytical and AI/ML ambitions of the business at scale.

Manual workarounds that had been embedded in operational processes for years were replaced with automated, monitored pipelines. The backlog-driven delivery approach allowed priorities to evolve as business needs became clearer, and the operational model established during the engagement ensured the platform continued to improve after the initial delivery phase.
