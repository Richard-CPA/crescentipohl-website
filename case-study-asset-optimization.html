Asset Optimization
Platform

Replacing legacy applications with a modular, cloud-based solution for power asset modelling and optimization — fully integrated into both current and target landscapes.

Context

Conventional and hydro generation portfolios represent stable, long-standing value streams for European utilities. Yet the systems built to optimize these assets — scheduling, dispatch, pricing — are often among the oldest in the IT landscape, predating cloud architecture and modern integration standards.

This utility faced a situation common across the industry: asset optimization running on monolithic legacy applications that could neither be extended to meet new analytical requirements nor realistically maintained in a stable operational state. The decision to replace was made; the challenge was doing so without disrupting the operating model that depended on it.

"Legacy optimization systems had become a strategic constraint — unable to meet new analytical demands, yet too critical to take offline without a fully integrated replacement."

Crescenti Pohl Advisory · Project observation

Client situation

Asset situation
  • Conventional and hydro generation assets providing a reliable and material value stream
  • Asset optimization running on legacy applications that could not be extended with new functionality
  • Existing systems increasingly difficult to maintain in stable operational condition
The core challenge
  • Need to replace business-critical optimization capability without operational disruption
  • Required seamless integration with both existing legacy landscape and the strategic target architecture
  • Analytical use cases — mid-term dispatch planning and long-term scenario modelling — each required different optimization approaches

Our approach

Legacy landscape & requirements analysis
Modular solution architecture design
Asset characterisation & parametrisation
Mid-term & long-term optimization delivery

Our contribution

Modular, flexible cloud-based solution architecture

Designed and delivered a cloud-based power asset modelling and optimization solution built on modular, extensible components — allowing capabilities to be added, replaced or scaled independently of each other.

Full integration into legacy and target landscapes

Ensured the new solution operated seamlessly within the existing operational processes and system interfaces, while simultaneously being architected to align with the client's strategic target IT landscape — avoiding a costly double migration.

Mid-term optimization for analytical use cases

Delivered a mid-term optimization module covering generation asset characterization and parametrisation, supporting analytical use cases such as hedging decisions, portfolio planning and regulatory reporting.

Long-term optimization with advanced scenario capability

Built a long-term optimization engine with highly advanced multi-scenario capability, enabling the client to model asset value across market, regulatory and technology scenarios at the strategic planning horizon.

Outcome

The client replaced a strategically critical but operationally constrained legacy system with a modern, cloud-based optimization platform — without disrupting day-to-day dispatch and portfolio management operations.

The modular architecture means individual components can be extended or replaced as the energy market evolves, addressing the root cause of the original constraint. Both mid-term analytical and long-term strategic planning capabilities are now supported natively, replacing manual workarounds and approximation models that had accumulated in the legacy environment.
